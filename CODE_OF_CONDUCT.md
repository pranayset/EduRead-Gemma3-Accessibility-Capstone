# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

EduRead aims to support accessible educational technology and responsible AI experimentation. Contributors and participants are expected to help maintain a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Welcome constructive feedback and differing viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not individuals.
- Respect accessibility needs, privacy, data rights, and licensing restrictions.
- Report mistakes and model limitations honestly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, intimidation, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this code. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer.
